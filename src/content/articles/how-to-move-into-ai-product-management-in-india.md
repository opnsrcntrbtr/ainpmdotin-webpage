## Is AI product management a real career path in India?

**Yes, and demand is measurable.** Naukri's [World AI Day Report 2026](https://telanganatoday.com/ai-skills-feature-in-15-4-per-cent-of-tech-job-descriptions-naukri-report) found AI skills in **15.4% of technology job descriptions**, up from **0.9% in 2020**, a roughly 17-fold rise across more than 300,000 job descriptions. LinkedIn's [AI Labor Market Report 2026](https://yourstory.com/ai-story/hiring-ai-professionals-india-rises-59-percent-linkedin-report) puts the growth of AI hiring in India at **59.5% year on year**. Naukri also lists product management among the functions where the largest share of professionals rely heavily on AI.

Demand does not mean an easy switch. Most AI PM roles go to people who can show they have already shipped or evaluated something.

## What skills do you need to switch into AI product management?

Six skills come up again and again in AI PM job descriptions:

1. **AI product strategy:** find where AI is clearly better than today's workflow, and where it is not.
2. **Evaluation:** turn requirements into a graded test set and quality gates. See [Evals are the new PRD](/articles/evals-are-the-new-prd/).
3. **Cost and model choice:** compare models on quality, latency and cost per task in rupees.
4. **Retrieval and agents with oversight:** citations, abstention, approvals and a kill switch.
5. **Regulation by design:** DPDP consent, RBI FREE-AI and SEBI expectations. See [India AI regulation for product managers](/articles/india-ai-regulation-for-product-managers/).
6. **Communication:** explain probabilistic results and trade-offs to people who want certainty.

## How do you move into AI product management, step by step?

1. **Choose a target role.** Collect five real job descriptions. List the skills they repeat and the domain they want.
2. **Learn the core skills.** Work through the six modules of the [free AINPM learning path](/learn/), about 24 hours over six weeks.
3. **Build one public project.** Ship something small with a golden set, results (including weak numbers), cost per task and a decision log. My [portfolio](/work/) shows the format.
4. **Map skills to evidence.** Write each skill as one sentence an employer would recognise and link it to proof, like my [skills matrix](/skills/).
5. **Practise interviews.** Rehearse product sense, an evaluation case and a regulation question out loud, with feedback.

If you are already a PM, start inside your company: volunteer for the AI feature nobody owns yet. An internal move is usually the fastest route.

## How should you choose an AI product management course?

Judge a course by what you can **show** at the end, not by the certificate. Ask five questions:

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Do you build and publish a real project? | Hiring managers check work, not syllabi |
| Does someone review your evaluations and decisions? | Feedback is what speeds up learning |
| Is the curriculum current on evals, agents and cost? | AI practice changes every few months |
| Are outcome claims verifiable? | Self-reported placement numbers are marketing |
| Does the credential name the skills it covers? | [Accredible (2026)](https://www.accredible.com/reports/2026-state-of-credentialing-report) found 44% of learners want clearer skill descriptions |

## Does a certificate get you an AI PM job?

**Rarely on its own.** Accredible's 2026 State of Credentialing research found that learners value credentials but career outcomes do not follow automatically. Its [2025 research](https://www.accredible.com/reports/2025-state-of-credentialing/hiring-needs-verifiable-proof) found **75% of HR and talent leaders** say digital credentials make skills easier to verify. The lesson: a credential helps most when it sits next to shipped work with measured results. I wrote more on this in [Proof over certificates](/articles/proof-over-certificates-ai-pm-skills/).
